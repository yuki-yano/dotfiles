how this page was made ▾

AI readers with different lives got this piece the way a person would: one passage at a time, no peeking ahead, free to quit whenever. A passage is a paragraph-sized cut, roughly 85 words; this piece became @@N@@ of them, and a script refused to show each reader the next passage until they wrote down what just happened in them.

The readers: @@READER_INTRO@@ Their real notes sit beside each passage with the needle they logged: +2 leaning in, -2 done. A dashed line means a reader stopped and never saw the rest. Afterward, a separate agent was quizzed with the piece taken away, holding only the reading notes; the "what stuck" lens shows what survived. None of this is a score. It's what happened to the readers, written down as it happened.
